# Production Quality Procedure

This synthetic procedure is provided only as demonstration data for the Manufacturing Knowledge Assistant.

## First-piece inspection
At the beginning of a production run, the first completed part must be inspected before normal production continues. Record the measured critical dimensions in the inspection record and compare them with the current drawing tolerances.

## Nonconforming product
When a part does not meet a specified requirement, identify it as nonconforming and move it to the designated quarantine area. Record the part number, work order, detected defect, quantity affected, and date. Do not return quarantined material to production without an approved disposition.

## Measurement equipment
Use only measurement equipment that is within its calibration period. Before use, visually inspect the instrument for damage and confirm that its identification matches the inspection record when traceability is required.

## Traceability
Production records must retain the work order, part number, inspection results, and applicable operator or station identifier so that inspected product can be traced to its manufacturing record.
