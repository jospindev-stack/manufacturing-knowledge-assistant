# Maintenance Safety Procedure

This synthetic procedure is provided only as demonstration data for the Manufacturing Knowledge Assistant.

## Required personal protective equipment
For routine mechanical maintenance in the production area, technicians must wear safety glasses, safety footwear with toe protection, and cut-resistant gloves. Hearing protection is also required when work is performed in a posted high-noise zone.

## Isolation before intervention
Before entering a guarded area or working on moving equipment, stop the machine and isolate hazardous energy according to the site's lockout procedure. Verify the zero-energy state before beginning the intervention. Never rely only on the machine's normal stop control as an energy-isolation method.

## Restart communication
After maintenance is complete, confirm that tools and temporary materials have been removed, guards are restored, and affected employees have been informed before energy is restored. Only authorized personnel may remove their lockout devices.

## Chemical cleaning
When an approved cleaning chemical is required, consult its safety data sheet before use and follow the specified handling, ventilation, and protective-equipment requirements.
