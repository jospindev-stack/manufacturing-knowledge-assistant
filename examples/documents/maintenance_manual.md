# MX-200 Preventive Maintenance Manual

This synthetic manual is provided only as demonstration data for the Manufacturing Knowledge Assistant.

## Pre-start inspection
Before restarting the MX-200 after maintenance, inspect all machine guards and confirm that every guard is secured. Remove tools, loose fasteners, cleaning materials, and other foreign objects from the work area. Verify that the emergency-stop circuit has been reset and that no person remains inside the guarded zone. The operator must then perform a visual inspection for leaks or damaged cables before enabling the machine.

## Lubrication schedule
The main conveyor bearing lubrication points must be serviced every 250 operating hours. Use the lubricant specified on the maintenance label and wipe the grease fitting before application. Do not mix incompatible lubricants.

## Belt inspection
Inspect the conveyor belt every 500 operating hours for cracks, frayed edges, abnormal wear, and incorrect tracking. Stop the machine and follow the applicable isolation procedure before making an adjustment.

## Abnormal vibration
If abnormal vibration appears during operation, stop production and record the machine state. Inspect fasteners, bearings, drive alignment, and rotating components before returning the equipment to service.
